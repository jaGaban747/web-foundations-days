The most difficult concept for me was how CSS decides which rule wins. In the Day 4 character counter, my counter turned bold when I went over 200 characters but never turned red. I could not see why until I learned that an id selector is more specific than a class selector, so the grey colour from the id rule kept winning over the red from my class. I fixed it by changing the selector. Now I check specificity first whenever a style refuses to apply, and I test one change at a time.
