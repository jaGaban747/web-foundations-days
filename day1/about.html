About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for writing down short notes the moment you think of them. It keeps your ideas in one place so you can read them again later.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your note into the text box.
  3. Click the Add Note button or press Enter.
  4. Read your saved notes in the Your notes list.

Features

  • Add a note in seconds
  • See how many notes you have
  • Works on phones and computers

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
Tab Move to the next field
Ctrl + R Reload the page
